    How a match works

    1. Each player places one marker, in random order. Later players see where the earlier ones went.
    2. The clock starts. Markers spread their color outward, and overlapping colors fight for the ground.
    3. Every resource you color pays you points each second. A contested resource splits its payout.
    4. Spend points on new markers or upgrades. When enemy color outweighs a marker's own Strength and Hold, it drains and then switches sides. Captures start 45 seconds in.
    5. Most points earned when time runs out wins. Cover the whole board in your color and you win at once.

    Bright mixed colors mark the contested ground. The stronger color takes most of the mix. Spending on new markers doesn't lower your score: only points earned count.

    Controls

    • When you can afford a marker, + marks light up inside your color. Click or tap one to place it.
    • Click or tap one of your markers to open its upgrades. Filled buttons are affordable. A ring around a button shows how close you are.
    • A small badge on a marker means it has an upgrade you can buy now.
    • On a phone, a strip under the board shows your time, points and next marker, with a Pause button.
    • 1 2 3 upgrade the open marker. Space pauses. Esc closes the menu.

    A solid ring in the attacker's color means a marker is being captured. A captured marker loses one Strength level and all its Hold, then is safe for 6 seconds (dotted ring).
